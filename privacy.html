NoteBean Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

NoteBean is a music theory practice app for beginners. We take user privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how NoteBean handles user information.

1. We Do Not Collect or Upload Personal Information

NoteBean does not require account registration or sign-in. It does not upload user information to the developer or ask users to provide a phone number, email address, real name, identification document, school, address, or other personally identifying information.

2. Information Stored Locally

To support multiple users practicing on one device, NoteBean stores the following information locally on the device:

  • Nickname
  • Avatar selection
  • Practice progress
  • Display language preference
  • Practice instrument preference

This information remains on the user's device. It is not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

3. We Do Not Use Sensitive Permissions

NoteBean does not access or use the following device permissions:

  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos
  • Contacts
  • Calendar
  • Bluetooth

NoteBean only uses the device's audio playback capability to play local practice notes, rhythms, and feedback sounds. It does not record audio.

4. We Do Not Track Users

NoteBean does not include advertising SDKs, track users across apps or websites, or collect advertising identifiers.

5. No Ads, Purchases, or Social Features

NoteBean does not include advertising, in-app purchases, chat, community features, user-generated content, or public sharing features.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete local profiles other than the one currently in use from within the app. Deleting the app will normally remove NoteBean data stored on the device. If the user restores the device from a system backup, data restoration depends on the iOS backup and restore process.

7. Third-Party Services

NoteBean does not currently integrate third-party analytics, advertising, sign-in, payment, or cloud synchronization services.

8. Children's Privacy

NoteBean does not ask users to provide their age, real name, school, or other identifying information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, show targeted advertising, or encourage users to make purchases.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If NoteBean adds accounts, cloud synchronization, network services, or other features that may affect privacy in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and disclose the changes on the App Store product page or within the app.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email:
